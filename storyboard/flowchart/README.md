### This directory contains flowchart used in [storyboard document](https://github.com/virtual-labs/ph3-exp-dev-process/blob/main/storyboard/README.org).
Flowchart: DLA Fractal Dimension Computation

This flowchart represents the logical sequence of the Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (DLA) simulation. It begins by initializing the simulation and placing a seed particle. Random particles are then generated and moved until they satisfy the sticking condition and attach to the existing cluster.

The process continues until the stopping condition is reached. The final DLA cluster is displayed, and the box-counting method is used to estimate its fractal dimension. Finally, the simulation results are presented to the user.
